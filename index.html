How a pitch night works

Every pitch follows the same format so ideas are easy to compare. It takes 25 minutes in total.

  1. 5 min

    The thesis

    What the company does and why the market has it wrong.

  2. 5 min

    Valuation

    The price you would pay and the numbers behind it.

  3. 5 min

    Risks

    What would make the thesis fail, and how you would notice.

  4. 10 min

    Questions and vote

    The room challenges the pitch, then votes to buy, hold off or pass.
