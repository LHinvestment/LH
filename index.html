Our history

LH began in 2017 as one of the founding student investment groups in the B&R Beurs Eindhoven competition. It won the champion title twice and retired in 2024 as the most successful group in the competition. In 2025, leading alumni founded LH+, moving beyond small competition portfolios to serious, research-led investing.

How a pitch night works

Each pitch is a thorough presentation built on fundamental analysis and financial data, supported by technical analysis.

  1. Step 1

    The thesis

    The business, its financial data and why the market has it wrong.

  2. Step 2

    Valuation

    Multiples, forecasts and the price we would pay, with technical analysis for entry.

  3. Step 3

    Risks

    Market sentiment, headwinds and what would invalidate the thesis.

  4. Step 4

    Questions and vote

    The group challenges the pitch, then votes to buy, hold off or pass.
